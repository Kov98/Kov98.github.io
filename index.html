05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

What kind of websites do you build?+

Business websites, landing pages, portfolio sites, and straightforward websites for restaurants, studios, and local services.

Will my website work on mobile?+

Yes. Responsive layouts are part of the build, with attention to navigation, readable text, and clear actions on smaller screens.

Can you work with clients outside your country?+

Yes. I work remotely and can discuss projects in writing in English. The project scope, delivery, and communication are agreed before work starts.

How much does a website cost?+

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design needs, content, and any extra features. A short project brief is the best starting point for a clear quote.
